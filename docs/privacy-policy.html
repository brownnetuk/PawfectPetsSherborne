Pawfect Pets Sherborne ("we", "us") provides dog walking, pet visits, day care and boarding in and around Sherborne. This policy explains what personal information we collect about you and your pets, why we collect it, how we keep it safe, and the choices you have. It covers our website, the Pawfect Pets Customer app, our online forms, and the information our staff hold about you in our booking system.

Who we are

What we collect

About you

  • Your name, email address, phone number and home address.
  • An emergency contact: their name, phone number, email and address. Please make sure they are happy for you to give us their details.
  • Your usual vet practice and your authorisation for us to arrange alternative veterinary care in an emergency.
  • Home access details you choose to give us so we can care for your pets while you are out: whether we hold keys, alarm or key-safe codes, and access notes. Alarm and key-safe codes are stored encrypted and are only revealed to staff when they need them for a visit.
  • Your signature, typed name and the date when you sign our client agreement, consent forms or other forms we send you.
  • Your login details for the customer app. Passwords are stored in hashed form. If you choose Face ID or Touch ID sign-in, the biometric check happens on your device only; we never receive your biometric data.

About your pets

  • Name, species, breed, sex, age, colour and markings, microchip number and insurance details.
  • Health information: vaccination status and expiry dates, a photo of the vaccination card if you upload one, medication, allergies, and whether your pet is neutered.
  • Behaviour and handling notes such as temperament, how your pet travels in the car, whether they can be let off the lead, and any consent you give for off-lead exercise.
  • Photos of your pets that you or our staff add.
  • Daily care notes, observations, feeding instructions and check-in and check-out records from boarding and day care stays.

About your bookings and payments

  • Quotes, bookings, invoices, credit notes and payment records: dates, services, amounts, and how you paid.
  • If you pay by card in person, the card is processed by SumUp. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • When we email you an invoice, we record when the email is opened so our staff know it has reached you.

From the apps and website

  • Messages you exchange with us through the customer app.
  • A device token so we can send you push notifications about invoices, quotes, bookings and messages. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Our website uses the cookies that are standard for WordPress. We do not run advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To provide our services: to care for your pets safely, follow your instructions, access your home when agreed, contact you or your emergency contact, and get veterinary help when needed. This is necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • To run the business: quotes, bookings, invoicing, taking payments and keeping accounts. This is necessary for our agreement with you and to meet our legal obligations.
  • To keep in touch: booking confirmations, reminders, pre-check-in forms, invoices and replies to your messages. This is part of providing the service.
  • To keep records safe and accurate: for example who checked a pet in and when, and signed consent forms. This is in our legitimate interests and, for some records, a legal requirement.
  • With your consent: push notifications, and any photos you choose to share with us.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Vets: your vet, or an alternative practice if your vet cannot be reached in an emergency, so your pet can be treated.
  • Service providers who help us run the apps and website, under contracts that protect your information: our hosting and database providers, our email delivery service, Apple (for push notifications), and SumUp (for card payments).
  • Your emergency contact, if we cannot reach you.
  • Anyone we are legally required to share information with.

Our systems are hosted in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. Where a provider processes data outside the UK, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your customer and pet records: for as long as you use our services, and for up to six years afterwards, so we can answer questions about past care and meet our accounting and legal duties.
  • Invoices and payment records: six years, as required by HMRC.
  • Messages and notifications: while you have a customer account with us.
  • Home access details: we delete alarm and key-safe codes when you ask us to, or when we stop caring for your pets.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner. We will do so unless we need to keep it for a legal reason, in which case we will tell you.

How we keep it safe

Our booking system and apps use encrypted connections. Access is limited to our staff, each with their own login, and sensitive details such as alarm codes are encrypted and only shown when needed. Staff devices are protected with passcodes and biometrics, and we can sign a device out remotely if it is lost.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong, or update it yourself in the customer app;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to a use;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it, for example by turning off push notifications.

To do any of these, email info@pawfectpetssherborne.co.uk or call 01935 717331. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

The customer app

Accounts in the Pawfect Pets Customer app are created by us for our clients; the app does not offer sign-up to the public. You sign in with your email address and a password you set using a code we email you. The app lets you view your invoices, quotes and bookings, keep your details, emergency contact, vet and pets up to date, and message us. You can delete your app account at any time in the app, under My Details, Delete Account, and you can ask us there to erase your personal data too. You can also contact us using the details above and we will do it for you.

Children

Our services and apps are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will let you know by email or through the customer app.
