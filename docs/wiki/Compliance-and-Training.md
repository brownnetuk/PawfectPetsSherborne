# Compliance and training

## Forms

**Settings > Forms** has a drag-and-drop form builder. Forms are used for pre-check-in, check-in, check-out, incident reports and anything else staff want customers or staff to fill in.

Field types include display text (or the live terms or declaration), today's date, date and time, text, long text, number, date, time, toggle, single choice, multiple choice, photo upload, medication list, signature and repeating groups. Forms can be split into pages and can use placeholders such as `{{customerName}}` or `{{firstName}}` once sent to a known customer.

Which forms are used for boarding and day care check-in and check-out, and whether they need a photo or signature, is set in **Settings > Boarding**. A form's PDF includes the terms and conditions as a numbered list.

## Risk assessments

**Boarding & DayCare > Risk Assessments.** Each assessment goes `draft → review → live`. Live assessments with a review frequency get a next review date, and staff are notified when it's due. **Review** moves the date on. Every change is kept in the assessment's audit trail. Risk assessments can be imported, exported as PDF, and attached to a customer.

## Policies

**Boarding & DayCare > Policies.** Policies are versioned. Publishing a version asks the chosen staff (or all active staff) to read and sign it; **Remind** nudges those who haven't. Only the latest version can be signed. Review dates notify staff when due.

## Checklists

**Boarding & DayCare > Checklists** and the staff app's **Boarding > Checklists**.

- Templates (Settings > Boarding) are for Boarding or Day Care, with items, an optional complete-by time and **Auto assign**.
- Auto-assigned templates are added to each day that has a matching booking.
- Staff tick items (the app records who), add notes and sign off.
- A reminder push goes out an hour before the complete-by time if items are still unticked.
- Export one checklist, a day, or the visible range to PDF.

## Staff training

| Page | For |
| --- | --- |
| Training Admin (`training.manage`) | Build modules from video (YouTube), reading and PDF blocks, plus a question bank. Set the pass mark, questions per attempt, who it's assigned to (or all staff), how long they have, and the review frequency. **Audit & Compliance** shows everyone's status. |
| My Training | Each staff member's assigned modules: work through the content, then take the quiz. Questions are picked at random from the bank on each attempt. |

Overdue training and review dates notify staff hourly.
